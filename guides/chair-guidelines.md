# Guidelines for Project Governing Board (PGB) Chairs

This document is designed to assist chairs of Project Governing Boards.


## PGB Chair Responsibilities

A Chair (or co-chairs) provides leadership for the PGB and is a neutral, impartial facilitator of the PGB’s work. The Chair task is to ensure that the group creates a common understanding of the goals of the project and that the group works efficiently to achieve those goals. The Chair should encourage and facilitate strategic discussions. The Chair makes sure that the Open Project is making progress.

The PGB chair should be familiar with the OASIS Open Project Rules – OASIS staff is there to assist with the interpretation and implementation.

### Convening and chairing PGB meetings

**Before the meeting:**
* Send an email to PGB mailing list 1 week prior to next meeting to ask for potential agenda items
* Drafting an agenda for each PGB meeting (together with Secretaries) and send it out 3-5 days ahead of each meeting;
* Identify clear goals for each meeting

**During a meeting:**
* Open each meeting with a review of the proposed agenda and ask for additions
* Make sure someone is tasked with taking minutes of meeting and posting them to the mailing list (or delegate this to other members of the WG)
* Take roll call to determine if quorum is reached
* Review and approve meeting minutes from previous meeting if quorum is reached
* If quorum is not reached, the meeting can still occur, but no decisions are to be made
* Keep the discussion on track and focussed on the meeting goals
* Drive the discussion but make sure to be inclusive
* Make voting proposals when appropriate
* Bring closure to agenda items and seek commitment for action items

See also [this guide for Meeting Facilitation](https://github.com/oasis-open-projects/documentation/blob/main/guides/planning-and-facilitating-meetings.md).

### Outside of Meetings
* Liaise with OASIS staff when necessary, for instance to ask for Special Majority ballots to be set up, guidance on best practices, or help with tools…
* Welcoming new members; per email or suggest a introductory call
* Support OASIS staff to recruit new sponsors and generally with outreach for the project (webinars, conference talks…) – this applies to all PGB members, not only the Chair

### Secretary Tasks
* Assist Chairs with drafting and agenda, and scheduling meetings
* Take minutes during the meeting or use Zoom/Webex/Teams automatic transcript functions and refine these transcripts too be published on Github and the OP mailing list
* During the meeting, keep an eye on the time, the chat window, and point out action items. The co-chair can also help to run votes. The committee may want to designate a Secretary for the project who can handle these functions.
* Note "raised hands" (and don't miss the "raised hand emoji") and keep a speakers list

### General Best Practices to Implement for the PGB:

**For meetings:**
* At least one Chair needs to be present in order to take a decision
* If no Chair is present, the meeting may occur, but no votes may be taken. Ideally, Chairs should communicate their absence in advance and ensure the meeting has a leader and agenda
* We suggest that the availability for the upcoming 1-2 meetings be reviewed in the current meeting
* Create a rolling document for meeting minutes and shared agenda planning. This way, you can take minutes collaboratively as well, or have people other than the Chair and Secretary add to the minutes (OASIS Staff can assist)
* Meeting minutes should be completed in a timely manner, filed appropriately, and sent to the PGB for review before the next meeting

**Outside of meetings:**
* Be proactive. Reach out to PGB members if you notice they do not attend meetings or do not seem involved during the meetings. OASIS staff can assist.
* Implement asynchronous work where possible. PGB meetings are best used as decision-making venues, but it is understood that newer projects need time to develop this process
* Delegate the work. People may not always volunteer, but most are willing to accept a task if asked.
* Send regular updates to group mailing list and to the public announce mailing list
* Add your chair position to your Linkedin profiles. This is a nice plug for the work, and every year, your contacts might get a reminder (work anniversary)
* Appoint someone from the group to onboard new members, for instance:
	* To have a conversation about why someone joined the OP; What their interests are; what they want to get out of OP membership
	* Explanation of the schedule for PGB meetings, intro to other committees/Workstreams
	* Intro to tools the PGB is using
* Work with OASIS event manager on getting talks about the project submitted - this applies to all PGB members, not only Chairs
